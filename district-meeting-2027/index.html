Privacy policy

How the meeting app handles information

Effective and last updated September 8, 2026 · App publisher and support contact: Javonn Liner · javonn19@gmail.com

01

Approved member access

Registration verifies the member’s email address. A super administrator must then approve the account before meeting information, updates, resources, or the private community can be opened.

02

No advertising or tracking

The current app contains no ad network, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, payment collection, or sale of personal information.

03

Private member community

Posts and shared photos are visible only to approved members. Suggestions are private to authorized organizers, and notification permission is optional.

Information collected

Only what is needed for access and meeting participation

  • Name, email address, account username, email-verification state, approval status, assigned roles, and internal account identifier
  • Authentication tokens stored in platform secure storage; the app does not retain the password after entry
  • Live meeting content published by authorized administrators, including the administrator username and update time in audit records
  • Member posts, selected photos, photo descriptions, author display name, internal account identifier, post time, and moderation records
  • Private suggestions, selected topic, account name and email, review status, and submission time
  • Optional device notification token, device platform, and alert preference when a member turns on push notifications
  • Selected photos may contain visual details or image metadata supplied by the device; members should not share sensitive personal, financial, health, or membership information
  • Security and operations metadata that hosting providers may process, including IP address, request time, logs, traces, and error details

How information is used

Meeting operations and member communication

  • Authenticate members and restrict the meeting app to approved accounts
  • Review registrations, assign roles, and provide approved meeting information, live updates, and venue logistics
  • Deliver announcements and optional push alerts
  • Display approved-member posts and photos, support author or administrator removal, and moderate meeting-inappropriate content
  • Provide downloadable resources and route private suggestions to authorized organizers
  • Secure, troubleshoot, and operate the service

Registration and approval

Verification does not automatically grant access

A person can request an account and verify the supplied email address, but the app remains locked until a designated super administrator approves the request. Rejected accounts cannot enter the app. Super administrators can review account details, approve or reject access, and publish live meeting updates.

Service providers and external links

AWS-hosted accounts, updates, and private content

Amazon Web Services provides identity, API, logging, tracing, email-delivery, account-recovery, live-content, database, and private photo-storage services. Expo provides device notification-token registration and push delivery after a member opts in. Google Maps and the Arizona Grand Resort site open only when you select their links and then operate under their own privacy terms. GitHub Pages hosts this policy and support page.

Disclosure and sharing

No sale of personal information

Information is not sold or shared for behavioral advertising. Member posts and photos are disclosed to approved members. Suggestions and account details are limited to authorized organizers. Information may also be disclosed to contracted service providers acting under instructions, or when reasonably required for security, legal compliance, or the protection of users and the service.

Retention

Limited by purpose

Cloud test accounts, approval records, test posts, test photos, suggestions, notification subscriptions, and live-content audit data are deleted or de-identified no later than 90 days after testing ends. Service logs are configured for 30 days. Production community posts, photos, and suggestions are deleted or de-identified no later than 90 days after the meeting ends unless they are needed to address a safety, conduct, security, or legal issue. Push subscriptions are removed when alerts are turned off, the member signs out, or an account is deleted, and are purged after the meeting. On-device authentication tokens remain until sign-out, expiry, app removal, or account deletion.

Security

Layered safeguards

Data is encrypted in transit, cloud tables use server-side encryption and point-in-time recovery, access is gated by email verification, administrator approval, and assigned roles, and private photos are stored in a non-public bucket and delivered through short-lived signed links, while uploads have type and size limits. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Audience

For adult meeting participants

The app is intended for adult meeting attendees, organizers, and authorized testers. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Artwork and local assets

Meeting artwork is bundled with the app

The district-meeting logo, fraternity shield, and temple illustration supplied by the organizer are packaged inside the app. Merely viewing these images does not contact an external image server, create an advertising identifier, or disclose personal information. The app does not use the images as tracking pixels.

Policy changes

If app features, sponsors, analytics, notification delivery, or voting practices change materially, this page will be updated with a new date before the changed behavior is released.

Voting is not in the MVP

The district currently uses Election Runner for district elections. The app’s main experience does not include voting. If an in-app voting feature is authorized later, the policy and store disclosures will be updated before members use it; any protected-voting records would keep participation identity separate from ballot selections.

Privacy requests

Access, correct, or delete your information

Email the address used for your meeting account with the subject “82nd District Meeting Privacy Request.” Include your username and the type of request, but never send your password, temporary password, access token, or verification code.

You can begin an account-deletion request from the Account page in the app or use the public email link below. Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed. Approved deletion requests include the account’s community posts, stored photos, feedback, and notification subscription unless a limited record must be retained for a safety, conduct, security, legal, or authorized future-election requirement.
