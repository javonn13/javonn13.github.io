Privacy policy

How the meeting app handles information

Effective and last updated September 8, 2026 · App publisher and support contact: Javonn Liner · javonn19@gmail.com

01

Open meeting guide

The cover, summary, agenda, speakers, venue diagram, and sponsor acknowledgements do not require an account.

02

No advertising or tracking

The current app contains no ad network, cross-app tracking, third-party analytics, payment collection, or sale of personal information.

03

Separated voting records

When protected voting is enabled, eligibility participation and ballot selections are stored separately to reduce identity-to-selection correlation.

Information collected

Only what is needed for access and voting

  • Name, email address, organizer-issued username, and internal account identifier
  • Authentication tokens stored in platform secure storage; the app does not retain the password after entry
  • Ballot ID and account identifier in the eligibility/participation record, without the selected option
  • Ballot ID, random receipt, selected option, and submission time in a separate vote record, without the member identifier
  • Security and operations metadata that hosting providers may process, including IP address, request time, logs, traces, and error details

How information is used

Meeting operations and election integrity

  • Provide approved meeting information and venue logistics
  • Authenticate eligible voters and administer accounts
  • Enforce one response per eligible account for each ballot
  • Count, review, audit, and certify authorized ballot results
  • Secure, troubleshoot, and operate the service

Demo beta behavior

No official vote is transmitted

The public testing build uses a demonstration ballot unless the organizers expressly enable the production cloud configuration. Completing the demo displays a confirmation on the device and does not record or transmit an official ballot response.

Service providers and external links

AWS-hosted voting when enabled

Amazon Web Services provides identity, API, database, logging, tracing, email-delivery, and recovery services for protected voting. Google Maps and the Arizona Grand Resort site open only when you select their links and then operate under their own privacy terms. GitHub Pages hosts this policy and support page.

Disclosure and sharing

No sale of personal information

Information is not sold or shared for behavioral advertising. It may be disclosed to authorized election administrators, contracted service providers acting under instructions, or when reasonably required for security, legal compliance, or the protection of users and the service.

Retention

Limited by purpose

Cloud test accounts and test ballot records are deleted or de-identified no later than 90 days after testing ends. Service logs are configured for 30 days. Official election records are retained through certification and any approved recount or challenge period, then deleted or de-identified within 90 days unless a longer organizational or legal requirement applies. On-device tokens remain until sign-out, expiry, app removal, or account deletion; receipts remain until app removal.

Security

Layered safeguards

Data is encrypted in transit, cloud tables use server-side encryption and point-in-time recovery, access is role-gated, and duplicate voting is enforced by a server transaction. No system can guarantee absolute security or absolute anonymity.

Audience

For adult meeting participants

The app is intended for adult meeting attendees, organizers, and authorized testers. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy changes

If app features, sponsors, analytics, notification delivery, or voting practices change materially, this page will be updated with a new date before the changed behavior is released.

Privacy requests

Access, correct, or delete your information

Email the address used for your voting account with the subject “82nd District Meeting Privacy Request.” Include your username and the type of request, but never send your password, temporary password, access token, or verification code.

Identity may need to be verified before a request is completed. Deleting an account does not silently delete a certified anonymous ballot, override an approved records requirement, or create a second chance to vote during an active ballot.
